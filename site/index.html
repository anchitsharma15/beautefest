A little more
good to know.

Can I buy skincare and makeup at BeauteFest?

Yes. Visit our store at LGF 94, Amayra Emporio, Kharar–Kurali Road to explore skincare, makeup, cosmetics and fragrances. Our team can help you browse the selections.

How can I check a product, shade or price?

Send the product name, brand, shade or size on WhatsApp, or call +91 99141 93973. Our team will confirm current availability and pricing.

Can I order and pay on this website?

This website helps you explore our categories and contact the store. Purchases, payment options and collection or delivery arrangements should be discussed directly with our team.

Can I get help choosing products in-store?

Yes. Come explore the range and ask our team for help with product choices, shades and your everyday beauty routine.

Do you also offer makeup services?

Yes. Bridal, engagement and ring-ceremony, party and reception, and pre-wedding makeup are part of our offering. Contact us to discuss your occasion and date.

When can I visit?

Please call or WhatsApp us to confirm current opening hours before visiting Amayra Emporio, Kharar.
