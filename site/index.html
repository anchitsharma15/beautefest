A few things you might wonder

Before we
say hello.

Which makeup services can I enquire about?

Bridal and wedding makeup, engagement or ring-ceremony looks, party and reception makeup, and pre-wedding makeup. Share your occasion with our team to discuss the look you have in mind.

How do I check prices and availability?

Send us your service, occasion date and any inspiration on WhatsApp, or call +91 99141 93973. Our team will confirm availability, pricing and what your appointment includes.

Can I explore beauty products in-store?

Yes. Visit BeauteFest at LGF 94, Amayra Emporio, Kharar–Kurali Road to explore makeup, skincare and fragrances. Contact us first if you’re looking for a specific product or shade.

Does sending an enquiry confirm my booking?

No. An enquiry starts a conversation. Your booking is confirmed only after you agree on availability and all appointment details directly with the team.

What should I share before my appointment?

Your occasion, date, preferred ready-by time and any look or outfit inspiration are a helpful starting point. Ask our team about preparation and any individual requirements before your appointment.

What are your opening hours?

Please call or WhatsApp our team to confirm current opening hours before visiting.
