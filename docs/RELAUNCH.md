## Terminology and research

“Normal makeup” is an informal salon label. “Classic (Traditional)” is clearer customer-facing wording for conventional application with brushes or sponges. HD is a product/finish approach aimed at camera appearance; airbrush is an application method using a device. They are not mutually exclusive scientific grades, and neither establishes a universal quality or wear-time ranking. A traditional look may use HD products. The client should explain how its Classic and HD packages differ in products, time or inclusions.

Workflow: client identifies stocked items → match exact Indian-market variants on official brand pages → draft factual descriptions and source links → client checks names, variants, prices, availability and images → publish only approved listings.
